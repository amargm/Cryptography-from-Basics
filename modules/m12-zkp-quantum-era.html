Zero-Knowledge & The Quantum Era

How do you prove you know a secret without revealing a single shred of the secret itself? And what happens when quantum supercomputers threaten to break the foundation of RSA?

2. The Quantum Threat: Shor vs. Grover

Quantum computers operate using quantum bits (qubits) that can exist in superpositions. Will they break all encryption?

Shor's Algorithm vs. Grover's Algorithm

What Breaks: Public-Key (RSA & ECC): In 1994, Peter Shor proved that a sufficiently large quantum computer can solve prime factoring and elliptic curves in minutes. When quantum computers arrive, current RSA and ECC will collapse.

What Survives: Symmetric (AES) & Hashes (SHA-256): Grover's quantum algorithm only speeds up brute-force searching by a square root ($O(\sqrt{N})$). AES-128 becomes equivalent to AES-64, but AES-256 still has $2^{128}$ quantum security, which remains unbreakable!

3. Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)

The security world is not sitting idly. Organizations worldwide (led by NIST) have standardized Post-Quantum Cryptography based on high-dimensional mathematical grids called Lattices.

Algorithms like ML-KEM and ML-DSA are already being deployed inside Google Chrome and Cloudflare to ensure our communications remain secure against tomorrow's quantum machines.
