The Big Picture

The Grand Unified Protocol: How All 13 Modules Connect

End-to-End Handshake Pipeline

When you connect to your bank, you don't use just one cipher. In a 200ms handshake, the entire curriculum works together in a choreographed defense-in-depth pipeline:

1. IDENTITY
Digital Certificates & PKI Chain of Trust
Verifying that the server is genuine using asymmetric digital signatures anchored to Root CAs.
Covered in: Module 09 (Signatures & PKI)
2. KEY AGREEMENT
Ephemeral Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange
Negotiating a one-time master shared secret over untrusted public Wi-Fi with forward secrecy.
Covered in: Module 08 (Public-Key & Diffie-Hellman)
3. DERIVATION
Key Expansion via HKDF
Expanding the raw shared secret into unique client/server read, write, and initialization vectors.
Covered in: Module 10 (Key Vaults & KDFs)
4. BULK TUNNEL
Authenticated Encryption (AES-256-GCM / ChaCha20)
Streaming application data at multi-gigabit speeds with continuous cryptographic tamper detection.
Covered in: Module 06 (Symmetric AES) & Module 07 (Hashing & HMAC)
5. CREDENTIALS
Hardware Vaults & Memory-Hard Passwords
Protecting user credentials using biometric Secure Enclaves and Argon2id password vaults.
Covered in: Module 10 (KDFs) & Module 11 (HTTPS, Tokens & Passkeys)
6. IDENTITY ANATOMY
X.509 Certificate Structures & Keystores
Deconstructing TBSCertificate fields, SAN domain extensions, PKCS#12 bundles, and canonical ASN.1 serialization.
Covered in: Module 13 (Connected Topics & X.509)
